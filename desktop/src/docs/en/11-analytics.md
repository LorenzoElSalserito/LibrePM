# Chapter 11: Analytics 📈

The **Analytics** page (its title is **"Analytics Hub"**) helps you understand your work patterns and how accurate your estimates are: "Productivity and estimate accuracy analysis".

---

## 🎛️ Project Selector

At the top right, a **project selector** lets you choose:
*   **All projects**: aggregated analytics across all your work.
*   **A specific project** (among non-archived ones): analytics for that project only. Some panels appear **only** in this mode.

---

## 🔢 Summary Cards (specific project)

When you select a project, four cards appear at the top:
*   **Total Tasks**: how many tasks the project has.
*   **Completed**: how many are done.
*   **In Progress**: how many are being worked on.
*   **Total Hours Logged**: the sum of the project's time entries (see Chapter 20, **Time Tracking**).

---

## 🔥 Focus Heatmap

The **Focus Heatmap** shows how much you focused day by day over the **last year**, calendar style: each square is a day, each column a week.
*   The deeper the green, the more focus minutes you logged that day (legend **Less** → **More**).
*   Hover over a square to see the date and minutes.
*   It helps you spot your most productive days and your consistency over time.

The data comes from **Focus Timer** sessions (the ▶ button on Kanban cards, Chapter 3). With a project selected, only sessions on its tasks are counted.

---

## 🎯 Estimate Accuracy

The **Estimate Accuracy** panel compares the estimated effort of tasks with the actual effort:
*   At the top, the **global average deviation** as a percentage: 🔴 red and positive if on average you took **longer** than planned, 🟢 green if you took less.
*   With **All projects**, the chart shows the average deviation (%) per project (up to 5 projects).
*   With a **specific project**, the chart shows the 5 most underestimated tasks, comparing estimated and actual minutes.

Only tasks with an estimated effort are counted, in projects you **own**. If there's no data you'll read "No estimate data available."

---

## 📊 Effort Variance (specific project)

The **Effort Variance** panel lists, for each estimated task in the project, the estimated hours ("est") and the hours actually logged ("act"), with two comparison bars and a badge with the percentage deviation:
*   🔴 red: over +20%.
*   🟡 yellow: from 0 to +20%.
*   🟢 green: at least 10% under the estimate.
*   ⬜ grey: on target.

If the project has no estimated tasks you'll read "No estimated tasks yet". This panel requires a selected project.

---

## ⏱️ Recent Time Distribution (specific project)

A bar chart with the hours logged on the **last 7 days on which you logged time** on the project (Time Tracking data, Chapter 20).

---

## 🧩 Tasks by Type and Completion Rate (specific project)

*   **Tasks by Type**: how many tasks there are of each type (Task, Milestone, Meeting...). It appears when the project has tasks of at least two different types.
*   **Completion Rate**: a ring showing the percentage of completed tasks.

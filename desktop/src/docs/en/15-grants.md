# Chapter 15: Grants & Stakeholders 📣

LibrePM has two pages dedicated to the people who fund and take part in a project:
*   **Grants**: grant calls, requirements, obligations and reporting periods.
*   **Stakeholders**: stakeholder register and map, sponsors, donors and partner organisations.

Like the Finance page, both work on the **active project** (chosen with the project selector in Planner, Kanban, Gantt or Charter, or by opening a project from **Projects**). Without projects they show *"Select a project"*.

> **⚠️ Warning:** On these pages the trash buttons delete the item **immediately, without asking for confirmation**.

---

## 📣 The Grants Page

At the top you'll find three tabs: **Calls**, **Obligations** and **Reporting**.

### Calls Tab
On the left is the **"Grant Calls"** list; on the right, the details of the selected call.

#### Creating a call
1. Click **"New Call"**.
2. LibrePM creates a call named "New call" and opens the edit form straight away.
3. Fill in the fields (each field is saved when you leave it):
    *   **Title**
    *   **Status**: Identified, Evaluating, Preparing, Submitted, Awarded, Rejected, Closed.
    *   **Issuer**
    *   **Deadline** (date)
    *   **Budget Available**
    *   **Description**
    *   **Reference Code**
    *   **URL**

Each call in the list shows its title, issuer, a coloured status badge and the deadline.

#### Viewing and editing
In the detail header you'll find:
*   **"Edit"** / **"View"**: switches between the edit form and the read-only view.
*   **"Delete"**: deletes the call.

#### Requirements
Below the details is the **Requirements** section, a checklist to verify whether you can apply:
*   Click **"Add"** to insert a "New requirement" of type **Eligibility**.
*   Tick the checkbox to mark it as met (the text is struck through).
*   The **Met: x/y** counter shows how far along you are.

> **📌 Note:** From the interface, requirements can currently only be added and ticked: their text and type (Document, Eligibility, Budget, Partner, Technical) cannot be edited and requirements cannot be deleted.

### Obligations Tab
Here you record the commitments that come with the grant or contract. Click **"Add"** to create a "New obligation", then fill in the table row:
*   **Title**
*   **Type**: Contractual, Grant, Regulatory, Reporting.
*   **Deadline** (date)
*   **Status**: Pending, In Progress, Completed, Overdue (you set it yourself: it does not change on its own when the deadline passes).

The trash icon deletes the obligation.

### Reporting Tab
Lists the **Reporting Periods** as cards. Click **"Add"** to create a "New period" running from today to 90 days from today. On each card you can:
*   Rename the period (click its name).
*   Change its status: Upcoming, Open, Submitted, Accepted.
*   Delete it with **"Delete"**.

> **📌 Note:** The start and end dates of a period cannot be edited from the interface.

## 🧭 The Stakeholders Page

At the top you'll find four tabs: **Stakeholder Map**, **Sponsors**, **Donors** and **Partners**. In every tab the **"Add"** button creates a new item that you then edit directly in the table cells (text is saved when you leave the field, dropdowns as soon as you pick a value).

### Stakeholder Map
Contains the **Influence / Interest Matrix** and the **Stakeholder Register**.

#### The matrix
A chart where each stakeholder appears as a circle with the first two letters of their name:
*   Vertical axis: **Influence** (Low, Medium, High, Critical, from bottom to top).
*   Horizontal axis: **Interest** (Low, Medium, High, from left to right).
*   The circle's colour depends on influence (green, light blue, yellow, red).
*   Hover to see name, influence and interest.

The four quadrants suggest how to handle them:
*   **Manage Closely** (top right: high influence, high interest)
*   **Keep Satisfied** (top left: high influence, low interest)
*   **Keep Informed** (bottom right: low influence, high interest)
*   **Monitor** (bottom left: low influence, low interest)

#### The register
Click **"Add"** to create a "New stakeholder". Columns: **Name**, **Organization**, **Role**, **Influence**, **Interest** and **Channel** (Email, Phone, Meeting, Portal). Changing influence or interest moves the circle in the matrix.

### Sponsors
For each sponsor: **Name**, **Organization**, **Email**, **Contact**, **Type** (Corporate, Individual, Foundation, Government, Other).

Below each sponsor is the **Commitments** section: click its **"Add"** to record a commitment with **Description**, **Amount**, **Currency**, **Status** (Proposed, Agreed, Active, Completed, Withdrawn) and **Agreement Date**.

### Donors
For each donor: **Name**, **Organization**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Type** (Individual, Foundation, Corporate, Government, Other).

Below each donor is the **Donations** section: with **"Add"** you record a donation with **Amount**, **Currency**, **Date**, **Notes** and the **Restricted** checkbox for earmarked funds.

### Partners
The **Partner Organisations** table is for consortium projects. Columns: **Name**, **Country**, **Role** (Coordinator, Partner, Associate, Subcontractor), **Contact** and **Budget Share**.

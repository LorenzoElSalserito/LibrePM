# Chapter 17: Portfolio Management 🗂️

The **Portfolio** page lets you group several projects into **programmes** and follow them together, with shared milestones.

Unlike Finance, Grants and Change Control, this page **does not depend on the active project**: it works across all your projects.

> **⚠️ Warning:** On this page the delete buttons act **immediately, without asking for confirmation**.

---

## 📚 Programmes

A programme is a set of related projects managed together to reach a common goal.

### Creating a programme
1. In the **Programmes** column on the left, click **"New"**.
2. LibrePM creates a "New programme" with status **Active**, with you as its owner, and selects it.

Each programme in the list shows its name and a status badge. If you have none yet, you'll see *"No programmes"*.

### Editing a programme
With a programme selected, at the top of its details you can:
*   **Rename it**: click the large name and type (saved when you leave the field).
*   **Change its status** from the dropdown next to the name: Active, On Hold, Completed, Archived.
*   **Delete it** with the **"Delete"** button on the right.

---

## 📊 Programme Summary

Below the header you'll find three cards:
*   **Projects**: how many projects belong to the programme.
*   **Milestones Completed**: how many of the programme's milestones are Completed.
*   **Total Milestones**: how many milestones the programme has.

---

## 📁 Programme Projects

The **Projects** card lists the projects that belong to the programme.

*   To add one, use the **"Add project..."** dropdown in the card header and pick the project: it is added immediately. The dropdown lists only projects not yet included and disappears once you've added them all.
*   The same project can belong to several programmes.
*   If the programme is empty you'll see *"No projects in this programme"*.

> **📌 Note:** For now, a project cannot be removed from a programme through the interface.

---

## 🚩 Programme Milestones

The **Milestones** card holds the milestones of the programme as a whole.

1. Click **"Add"**: LibrePM creates a "New milestone" with status **Pending**.
2. Fill in the table:
    *   **Name**
    *   **Target Date**
    *   **Status**: Pending, In Progress, Completed, Overdue (you set it yourself: it does not change on its own when the date passes).
3. The trash icon deletes the milestone.

Milestones are sorted by target date when you reopen the programme.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Go to the **Portfolio** page.
2. Click **"New"** and give the programme a name.
3. Add projects with **"Add project..."**.
4. Define the programme milestones with target dates.
5. Update their statuses and keep an eye on the summary counters.

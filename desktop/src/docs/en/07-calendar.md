# Chapter 7: The Calendar 📅

The **Calendar** page gives you a traditional calendar view of the tasks of the **current project**. It's the best way to see what's happening on a given day, week, or month.

> **💡 Note:** The Calendar has no project selector of its own: it shows the current project. To change it, use the selector in Planner, Kanban or Gantt, or open a project from the Projects page. If no project is selected you'll see *"Select or create a project to view the calendar."*

---

## 🗓️ What appears on the Calendar

The Calendar has no separate events: every item is a **task** of the project (archived tasks are excluded).
*   If the task has a **Planned Start** and **Planned Finish**, it appears as a bar covering every day from start to finish.
*   If it only has a **Deadline**, it appears as a single-day item on that date.
*   Tasks with neither planned dates nor a deadline don't appear.

When a day has too many items, a "+ N more" link appears: click it to see them all.

---

## 🗓️ Calendar Views

The Calendar supports four different views:
*   **Month**: See the whole month at a glance.
*   **Week**: Focus on one week with more detail per day.
*   **Day**: Dive into a single day.
*   **Agenda**: A simple list of the items, sorted chronologically.

Switch between views using the buttons in the calendar toolbar. The same toolbar has **Back**, **Today** and **Next** to move through the displayed period.

---

## 🧭 Navigation

The header at the top gives you precise control over navigation:

*   **Month Selector**: A dropdown listing all months (in your language). Pick any month to jump straight to it.
*   **Year Selector**: A dropdown with years (current year ± 10). Switch between years instantly.
*   **Today Button** (📅): Click to return to today's date immediately.
*   **Refresh Button** (🔄): Reloads the tasks from the server.

---

## 🎨 Color Coding

*   🔴 **Red**: Tasks whose deadline has passed and that are still open (**Done** or **Cancelled** tasks are never overdue).
*   🟢 **Green**: **Done** tasks.
*   🔵 **Blue**: **In Progress** tasks.
*   ⬜ **Grey**: All other tasks (To Do, Review, Blocked, Cancelled).

Clicking a task in the calendar shows its **status** and **priority** as coloured badges in the side panel; the **Overdue** statistic only counts tasks that are still open.

---

## ➕ Creating a Task from the Calendar

Click a day (or select a range) in the calendar to create a new task:

1. The **Task Editor** opens ("New Task", see Chapter 3) with the dates already filled in: **Planned Start** on the clicked day, **Planned Finish** and **Deadline** on the following day.
2. Enter at least the **Title**. If you like, in the **Type** field choose for example **Meeting**, **Call**, **Appointment** or **Task Block**.
3. Click **"Create Task"**.

The task will appear on the calendar (and also in Planner, Kanban and Gantt).

---

## 📋 Viewing Details

Click an item on the calendar to see its **title** and **date** in the **right panel**.

To edit the task, open it from the Planner or the Kanban board.

---

## 📊 Calendar Statistics

The right panel (**Task Calendar**) also shows a **Statistics** box:
*   **Events on calendar**: How many of the project's tasks appear on the calendar.
*   **Tasks with deadline**: Total number of tasks that have a deadline set.
*   **This month**: How many tasks fall in the displayed month (based on the planned start or, if missing, the deadline).
*   **Overdue**: How many tasks are past their deadline.
